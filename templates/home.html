TOLL MANAGEMENT AND SPEED PREDICTION USING ARIMA

Traffic congestion and delays at toll plazas are a persistent problem. The project has been designed for the automation of toll tax payments by calculating the distance travelled by the vehicle.This project aims at designing a system, which automatically identifies the vehicle when it enters the Toll-Zone and initiates the system to process the distance travelled and the automated deduction of Toll tax.
